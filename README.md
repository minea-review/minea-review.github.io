# Minea Field Notes

Minea is an ad spy tool aimed at dropshippers. Here is what the homepage claims, what you can verify before paying, and where the workflow runs out.

**Read the full page:** https://minea-review.github.io/

If you sell physical products and your day starts with scrolling Meta ads, Minea puts that scrolling in one searchable place and adds shop-level numbers on top. If you are a marketer who needs to watch a named list of competitors over months, or who needs creators to run those ads, the fit is looser. The one caveat is that nothing on the public homepage tells you the price, so you cannot budget before you hand over an email. For competitor ad monitoring and creator discovery without the dropshipping framing, Reachara covers that narrower job.
